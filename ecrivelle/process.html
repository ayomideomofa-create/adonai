My process

How a project moves from idea to finished book.

Six clear stages, the same for every project. You always know where your manuscript is and what happens next.

→
  1. 1

    Discovery and consultation

    We start with a conversation about your book, your goals and your readers. You share what you have — an idea, notes or a full draft.

    • A free initial chat or written brief
    • Honest advice on the service you actually need
  2. 2

    Understanding the requirements

    I review your manuscript or materials closely and confirm the scope, word count, style guide, platform and deadline.

    • A written scope and quote
    • An agreed timeline with milestones
  3. 3

    Planning and research

    For writing projects I build an outline and research the subject; for editing I map the manuscript; for formatting I set up the template and specs.

    • Outline, edit plan or layout template
    • Style sheet for consistency
  4. 4

    Writing, editing or formatting

    The core work, delivered in stages so you can review as we go — chapters, edit passes or layout proofs.

    • Progress updates at each milestone
    • Tracked changes and comments you can review
  5. 5

    Quality assurance and revisions

    I check the work against the brief and style sheet, then refine it based on your feedback within the agreed revision rounds.

    • Final consistency and accuracy checks
    • Agreed revision rounds included
  6. 6

    Final delivery

    You receive clean, final files in the formats you need, plus guidance for uploading or submitting them.

    • Final files (DOCX, PDF, EPUB, KDP-ready)
    • Upload or submission guidance

Timelines depend on the project.

A proofread of a short ebook can take days; a ghostwritten book takes months. You'll get a specific timeline with your quote, before you commit to anything.
